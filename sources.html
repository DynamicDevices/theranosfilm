05 / Evidence and accountability

Sources are the start.
Not the conclusion.

A source can be reliable about one thing and insufficient for another. This register explains the distinction. Updated 21 November 2026.

Official role/verdict reference located through search; full-page review pending. Do not extrapolate his findings to other people.

Peer-reviewed study: abstract and selected methods passages inspected. 60 healthy adults and 22 tests. Study scope is not every assay, patient population or proprietary instrument. Full methods, limitations and conflict-of-interest review remains necessary.
Theranos’s 2016 technology announcement
Company statement, retained from earlier research. Evidence of the company’s presentation, not independent performance validation.
Patent US8283155B2
Patent-record starting point. Full claims, ownership and implementation audit unfinished. No clinical inference follows merely from grant or publication.

How conclusions should be labelled

Use document wording, participant account, legal allegation, reported verdict, scientific study, interpretation or open question. Explain the limitation at the claim, not only in a disclaimer elsewhere. A source count is not a truth score.

Corrections and contributors

Open a source-backed issue. Give the page, exact statement, proposed correction and supporting passage. Contributions are proposals until reviewed. Preserve who submitted, who reviewed, what changed and the exact revision. Never post confidential or sensitive personal material.

The graph currently uses a small curated set of relationships; it does not represent a complete evidence database. Structured imports, individual review receipts and a published correction ledger remain implementation work.
