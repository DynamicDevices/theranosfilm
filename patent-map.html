The patents were asserted after closure—but value was not adjudicated

Labrador's March 2020 complaint asserted US8283155B2 and US10533994B2 against BioFire/bioMerieux, alleging ownership by assignment. A subsequent notice dismissed the case without prejudice. These records establish an assertion and dismissal, not proven infringement, a completed title-chain audit, royalties, damages, clinical utility or a sale valuation. The plaintiff's explanations for dismissal remain its account.

A separate granted optical design—not a diagnostic certificate

The USPTO's 10 November 2026 record identifies US12791485B2, Raman spectroscopy with counterpropagating pump and Stokes beams, assigned to Haemanthus and naming Joseph G. LaChapelle and Roger S. Cannon. This is distinct from application US20250277701A1. It establishes a particular granted design, not a clinically validated replacement for Theranos, comprehensive legal title, freedom to operate or identification of the film engineer.

Recorded transactions narrow the ownership question

The Google Patents event list for US8283155B2 records a December 2017 assignment to Theranos IP Company and a separate Fortress security interest, a 2020 name change to Labrador Diagnostics, and July 2023 assignments through Golden Diagnostics Top Corp. to Golden Diagnostics Corp. These are database-recorded events, not inspection of the underlying instruments or a current legal-title opinion. A security interest must not be called an outright ownership transfer.

The granted claim's centre

Claim 1 concerns a two-way communication system: a reader, an external protocol source and an identified test device with a sample collector and assay reactants. Dependent claim 9 adds collection below about 500 microlitres. That limit is not itself a claim that all tests run from one drop.

Ownership and dates need their own evidence

The record lists Theranos as original assignee and 9 October 2012 as the grant publication date. Later database ownership labels do not establish legal title at every investment date. Check the assignment instruments and prosecution history before making an ownership, validity or freedom-to-operate conclusion.
