Which kind of partner?

Executive, romantic, investment and creative relationships are different. Dates and documented responsibilities matter more than the shared label.

Elizabeth Holmes's father; former Enron Clean Energy Solutions Group vice president

Christian R. Holmes

Sourced background
Rice University's professional biography describes a vice-presidential role concentrating on environmental technology and energy efficiency: fuel cells, waste-to-energy, pollution control and emissions allowances.
Involvement
This institutional source narrows the Enron role beyond the generic description of an executive. It does not establish responsibility for Enron's accounting or financing structures.
Connection and its limits
A family relationship and employment at another failed company are biographical connections, not evidence of involvement in either company's fraud.
Editorial interpretation
Keep employment, operational remit, knowledge and misconduct separate. No evidence connecting him to Enron's accounting fraud was found in this bounded research pass; that is not a comprehensive exoneration or a finding that all his work was investigated.
Still unverified
A complete dated employment history and any relevant primary conduct records have not been assembled. Do not infer guilt by association or diagnose family motives.

Theranos founder and chief executive; central subject of the film

Elizabeth Holmes

Sourced background
The SEC's 2018 announcement identifies Holmes as Theranos's founder and CEO. Her corporate role makes dated representations about the company's technology and business relevant to this investigation.
Involvement
The festival synopsis describes documentary access before her incarceration. A later account of a past capability must be compared with evidence available at the earlier date, not treated as a substitute for it.
Connection and its limits
Led the company in which Balwani held senior operational roles. The later domestic setting with Billy Evans is a different relationship and period.
Editorial interpretation
Test specific statements, knowledge and dates. Neither a sympathetic portrayal nor an uncomfortable interview overturns a legal finding. The DOJ reported four investor-related convictions in January 2022, alongside acquittals and counts without a verdict.
Still unverified
A checked film transcript, dated technical responsibilities and a statement-by-statement comparison remain incomplete. Visible nodding cannot establish dishonesty or a diagnosis.

Former Theranos president and chief operating officer; separately tried defendant

Ramesh ‘Sunny’ Balwani

Sourced background
The DOJ describes Balwani as a senior Theranos executive. Its July 2022 announcement reports two conspiracy convictions and ten wire-fraud convictions involving investors and patients.
Involvement
His operational role belongs to the company history under examination. That does not establish which film sequences discuss him; a checked scene record is still needed.
Connection and its limits
Executive colleague of Holmes. His verdict came from a separate trial and must not be substituted for hers, or hers for his.
Editorial interpretation
Investigate responsibility through dated organisational roles, communications and specific decisions. A general label such as partner is too imprecise to allocate responsibility.
Still unverified
A complete relationship chronology, disputed accounts and operational decision map have not been assembled here. The conviction announcement is not the full trial or later appellate record.

Holmes's romantic partner in the festival's account of the film

William ‘Billy’ Evans

Sourced background
The festival listing identifies Evans in the domestic setting. This provides a limited, attributed starting point—not a verified biography or technical credential.
Involvement
His presence can give viewers access to Holmes's later private life. Assess what he actually says and what period it concerns before drawing a business connection.
Connection and its limits
A later personal relationship with Holmes does not establish a role in earlier Theranos fundraising, laboratory operations or representations.
Editorial interpretation
Domestic testimony may illuminate personal experience while leaving technical or financial questions unanswered. Those are different subjects, not inherently contradictory accounts.
Still unverified
Independent background checks, exact scene statements and any documented business involvement remain research questions. No wrongdoing is inferred from the relationship.

Co-director; the interviewer identified in this research

Nathan Fielder

Sourced background
The festival credits Fielder alongside Lance Oppenheim. An indexed HBO publicity listing associates him with Nathan For You and The Rehearsal; direct access to that publicity page was blocked during this pass.
Involvement
Oppenheim says Holmes approached A24, Fielder initially intended to produce, then became co-director and was drawn into interviewing on camera. This narrows the origin question through a participant account, not independent commissioning records.
Connection and its limits
Works with Oppenheim on the film and with performer Amanda Seyfried in its production context. Professional collaboration does not establish a shared view of every disputed claim.
Editorial interpretation
His earlier work is a reason to examine staging, awkward pauses, constructed situations and uncertainty about sincerity. That is a viewing hypothesis, not proof that a particular scene is staged or that his intention was to deceive.
Still unverified
The A24 agreement, independent confirmation of the origin account, final-cut authority and exact interview protocol remain unverified. His comedy background does not establish a commissioning motive.

Actor involved in the film's performance element

Amanda Seyfried

Sourced background
The participant interview identifies Seyfried's involvement and discusses performance. It is an edited participant account, not a complete verification of what every shot depicts.
Involvement
Fielder says Seyfried was approached after the Holmes/Evans filming and shown what became the first half. Her recreations address off-camera conversations; they are not contemporaneous recordings of Holmes speaking.
Connection and its limits
Collaborates within the film's production. Playing or interpreting a person does not make the performer a witness to the earlier business events.
Editorial interpretation
For each passage distinguish voice, visible performer, script source, reconstruction notice and any documentary insert. Similar appearance or delivery is not a reliable identification method.
Still unverified
The exact boundaries between recording and performance, source script and scene timestamps still need a checked film copy or authenticated transcript.

Compare accounts, not personalities

For each disputed statement record speaker, date, audience, exact wording, source and counterevidence. Do not diagnose a person from appearance, attribute guilt through family or professional links, or infer a filmmaker's motive from their genre.

Begin with present-versus-future technology claims.
