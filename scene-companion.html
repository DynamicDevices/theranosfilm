The reconstruction has a different evidence status

Oppenheim says later reenactments used scripts based on the filmmakers' memory notes, while describing the filmed encounters as unscripted reactions. That is an attributed production claim, not our audit of rushes or scripts. A memory-based performance is not a contemporaneous recording of the reconstructed exchange.

How to test the film's present-versus-future argument

For each relevant scene, record the exact statement, speaker, edition and surrounding question. Compare it with the dated investor record, not a later prototype. Our investor chronology separates 2013 operating claims, 2014 forecasts, 2015 limited clearance and 2018 engineering results. Without a checked passage we cannot conclude that the film contradicts, explains or omits a particular record; absence from our notes is not proof of absence from the film.

Nodding is observable; its cause is not established

A checked recording could establish when a nod occurs and which question, pause or edit surrounds it. It cannot by itself establish lying, agreement, a medical condition or a psychological motive. Compare complete exchanges, camera continuity and possible listening behaviour before interpreting a gesture. No frequency count or behavioural diagnosis has been made here.

Is the first half Holmes or an actress?

In the 11 November 2026 participant interview, Fielder describes filming at the beach house with Holmes and Evans, then approaching Seyfried roughly two years later and showing her what became the first half. Seyfried describes around 90 minutes of footage. Fielder says her recreations concerned initial conversations that happened off-camera. This supports genuine Holmes footage followed by a film also using performance—not a secretly actor-played entire first half. It remains the participants' edited account, not independent verification of each shot or a timestamp boundary.

Scene-note template

  1. Identify the lawful viewing source, edition, stated runtime and date watched.
  2. Define zero time: distributor cards included or excluded; record start and end.
  3. Separate exact quotation, paraphrase and recollection. Include surrounding question and qualifications.
  4. Identify visible speaker, voice, source of reconstructed words and any production disclosure separately.
  5. Give a short necessary description and source locator, not the film or an unauthorised transcript dump.
  6. Mark your note proposed; another editor records any decision against its exact revision.
