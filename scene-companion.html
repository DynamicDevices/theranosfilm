Is the first half Holmes or an actress?

In the 11 November 2026 participant interview, Fielder describes filming at the beach house with Holmes and Evans, then approaching Seyfried roughly two years later and showing her what became the first half. Seyfried describes around 90 minutes of footage. Fielder says her recreations concerned initial conversations that happened off-camera. This supports genuine Holmes footage followed by a film also using performance—not a secretly actor-played entire first half. It remains the participants' edited account, not independent verification of each shot or a timestamp boundary.

Scene-note template

  1. Identify the lawful viewing source, edition, stated runtime and date watched.
  2. Define zero time: distributor cards included or excluded; record start and end.
  3. Separate exact quotation, paraphrase and recollection. Include surrounding question and qualifications.
  4. Identify visible speaker, voice, source of reconstructed words and any production disclosure separately.
  5. Give a short necessary description and source locator, not the film or an unauthorised transcript dump.
  6. Mark your note proposed; another editor records any decision against its exact revision.
