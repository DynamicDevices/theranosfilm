04 / Money and failure

Follow the money.
Don’t invent the ledger.

We do not yet have a verified reconciliation of Theranos’s spending. That is a research gap, not permission to fill the page with a persuasive but unsupported chart.

First, define the number

Funding raised, company valuation, revenue, operating losses and cash spent are different quantities. A valuation is not money received. Accounting losses may include non-cash items. A forecast is not an actual result. Even a valid financing total needs dates and a definition of which rounds it includes.

The 2018 SEC complaint describes more than $700 million raised in the late-2013–2015 period. Do not relabel that scoped figure as an audited lifetime total or as the amount personally received by Holmes.

What a proper reconciliation needs

  1. Beginning cash, financing receipts and other actual cash inflows.
  2. Actual expenditure grouped by documented categories and reporting periods.
  3. Investments in equipment and other assets, with accounting and cash effects distinguished.
  4. Debt movements, asset disposals and other material outflows or inflows.
  5. Ending cash and an explanation of differences or missing records.

Research and development, payroll, facilities, commercial operations, legal costs and administration are possible categories to investigate—not confirmed proportions of Theranos expenditure. Any allegation of personal enrichment needs its own transaction evidence.

Why failure has several meanings

A technology can fail technical validation. A service can fail patient-quality requirements. A business can fail to generate a sustainable return. Management can misrepresent capabilities. These outcomes can coexist, but one does not automatically establish the others.

Failed research is not inherently theft. A promising research programme is not a defence for a false claim that it is already proven. Our analysis must test both the genuine work and the representations made about it.

What we will not claim yet

No spending pie chart, unexplained “missing money” total or assertion about where every investment dollar went. We need authenticated historical financial statements, cash-flow records and relevant court exhibits. Projections and similarly numbered exhibits from different trials cannot be substituted.
