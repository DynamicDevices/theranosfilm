How to verify a scene

  1. Record the edition, total runtime and your timecode convention (including whether distributor cards are counted).
  2. Record the speaker and exact words only after checking the recording; otherwise label a recollection or paraphrase.
  3. Identify camera footage, inserted archive, performance and reconstructed speech separately. Note captions and disclosed reconstruction sources.
  4. Compare the passage with a dated source. Preserve surrounding questions, edits and qualifications.
  5. Submit a locator and short necessary description, not an unauthorised copy of the film.

Nodding is an observation, not a verdict

A useful coding exercise would count visible nods in a defined passage, identify cuts and listening/speaking context, and state uncertainty. It cannot establish dishonesty, diagnosis or motive. Read the evidence limits.
