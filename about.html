Purpose, method and participation

Research you can
trace and challenge.

We are building an independent, public-source investigation of Theranos and its portrayal in You Can See Everything. The aim is to make specific claims understandable, traceable and open to correction—not to endorse a film, diagnose its subjects or invite suspicion through association.

The research question

How do the accounts of Elizabeth Holmes, her partners, the interviewer and the actress compare with contemporaneous evidence? What did the technology actually do, what was represented to investors, and how does the film explain the difference?

Why specialists may find it useful

The case offers questions about technical due diligence, misleading representations, organisational incentives and the way edited accounts influence belief. These may interest UK security, fraud prevention, regulatory, scientific and journalistic professionals. Relevance to those disciplines does not establish a national-security connection or any official interest in this project.

Current assessment and limits

The project has a preliminary evidence map and guides to the people, technology, investors and money. Programme listings and a participant interview establish several identities and production context; an independent scene audit is unfinished. Investor documents offer testable examples of capability claims. Wording alone does not prove performance, receipt, reliance or intent. No complete financial reconciliation or clinical-validation audit has been completed.

We do not claim privileged access, an investigative mandate, official endorsement or a relationship with any UK agency. This is public-interest research, not an official intelligence assessment.

What a useful contribution looks like

  • A narrowly stated correction or question, not a general assertion about someone’s character.
  • An original public source, exact page or timestamp, date and explanation of what it supports.
  • A clear distinction between direct observation, reported information and interpretation.
  • Relevant qualifications, competing explanations and evidence that challenges the proposed conclusion.
  • Public account attribution, licence consent and an auditable review record.

Specialists can help with film/production context, assay science, patent interpretation, corporate records and evidential reasoning. Expertise is welcome; credentials do not replace sources. Contributor identity and conflicts of interest should be represented accurately without exposing unnecessary personal information.

Engagement and safety

Use the project’s public issue tracker for source-backed questions and corrections. Contributions can open a discussion with the project, but do not guarantee a response or establish a private relationship. The structured submission and review service is still being built.

Do not post confidential, classified, unlawfully obtained or sensitive personal material. This public repository is not a secure reporting channel. Do not submit private contact details or use it for urgent threats. Any private contact route would need to be explicitly established and approved separately.

Start with the people-focused guide, inspect the evidence connections, or read the contribution method.

Contribute something that can be checked

Prepare a source-backed JSON proposal or open an evidence issue. Keep a precise locator, support and limitations together; proposals are not automatically accepted.
