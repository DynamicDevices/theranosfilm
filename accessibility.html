Read, navigate and question the evidence

The evidence should remain available without graphics, scripts or a particular way of navigating.

Reading and navigation

  • The film guide, evidence comparisons, timeline, technology explanation and contribution history are static HTML. They remain readable with JavaScript and WebGL disabled.
  • Use the skip link at the beginning of each page to reach the main content. Headings and permanent fragment links let you share individual questions.
  • The optional claim search announces the result count. Clear the search to restore all entries; opening a claim fragment restores its entry if a filter hid it.
  • The map has readable evidence entries. Positions, colour and connecting lines are not the only way to understand a relationship or its status.
  • Print styles produce a reading copy. Forms and search controls are optional; GitHub's native evidence form is available without this site's JavaScript.

What has been tested

Structural automated checks cover page titles, links and fragment targets, source references, unsafe URL handling, schema formats, review-to-proposal hashes, append-only records and release metadata. On 21 November 2026, browser checks covered 390-pixel and 1280-pixel layouts, keyboard skip-link access, claim filtering and direct links, script-free homepage/claim reading, reduced-motion behaviour and safe local JSON preparation. Phone map-target overlaps were found and corrected; the checked phone targets exceeded 44 pixels in height. No page-level horizontal overflow was found in the checked new pages.

Homepage motion and the latest audit

The optional homepage video is decorative illustration, not film footage. It has a static poster and a pause control. Reduced-motion and Save-Data preferences prevent automatic video loading; lifecycle tests cover manual pause, offscreen/hidden playback and failed or blocked media.

On 21 November 2026, axe-core 4.14.1 checked all 21 release pages in headless Chrome 158. The initial homepage check found a visible-label/accessibility-name mismatch in the wordmark; this was corrected. The subsequent full-site check reported zero automated violations. Two map labels were given solid backgrounds and passed targeted rechecking. Axe still marked a decorative arrow for manual contrast consideration because it contains only a non-text character; it is hidden from assistive technology and repeats the link’s written direction. Its foreground and paper-background colours were checked separately.

An isolated Chrome keyboard test verified the skip link and entry into main content, visible focus and Space activation of the video pause control. The homepage, claims and contribution pages had no page-level horizontal overflow at a 320 CSS-pixel viewport. A browser reduced-motion test confirmed that the poster remained visible, no video source was attached and no video request was made. A 390-pixel browser check also confirmed a circular crop and no page overflow. These checks describe the local release-shaped preview, not every browser or assistive technology.

What is not claimed

This is not a WCAG certification or a substitute for testing with disabled readers. Automated checks cannot establish screen-reader usability, clear language or a fully accessible WebGL map. Full screen-reader and real-device coverage remain unverified for this release. Report a barrier with the page, device/browser, assistive technology if relevant and what you expected—without sharing private information.
