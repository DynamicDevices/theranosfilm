From expertise to a recorded finding

  1. Contributor proposes one correction with an exact source locator and limitations.
  2. Maintainers check the actual GitHub provenance, declared interests and relevant source.
  3. A separate reviewer records a decision bound to the proposal's exact hash and discussion/revision.
  4. The original proposal, disagreement and later corrections remain visible, subject to privacy handling.

Expertise helps evaluate a source; it does not replace it. Editorial acceptance remains open to challenge.
